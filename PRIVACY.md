# Privacy Policy

FullPage Shot does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- Screenshots are captured and stitched entirely inside your browser and saved to your computer through Chrome's downloads.
- No data is sent to any server, and the extension contains no analytics or tracking.
- The extension only runs on the tab you are viewing, and only when you click **Capture Full Page**.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Pouya-Mansournia/fullpage-shot/issues
